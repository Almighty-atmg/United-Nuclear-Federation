United Nuclear Federation 9-K

Official Digital Codex & Rules of Engagement

Preamble

We, the members of the United Nuclear Federation of 9K, in order to establish order, ensure maximum interaction, and promote absolute freedom of banter within our digital boundaries, do ordain and establish this Constitution.

Article I: Jurisdiction
Sec. 1 This group is officially the United Nuclear Federation of 9K. These rules apply to all members and visitors.
Sec. 2 This Constitution is the supreme law of the chat. No decree shall contradict it.
Article II: Membership
Sec. 1 Members hold the right to participate freely within constitutional limits.
Sec. 2 Silence is discouraged. Members must contribute memes, opinions, or chaos to keep the Federation alive. (Can be suspended during Emergency/War)
Article III: Administration
Sec. 1 Admins act as the executive branch to enforce rules, mediate disputes, and maintain security.
Sec. 2 Admins shall not abuse removal powers without just cause.
Article IV: Rights
Sec. 1 Freedom of banter is protected, including roasting and unhinged humor, barring targeted harassment.
Sec. 2 The sharing of quality memes and stickers is fully protected under Federation culture.
Sec. 3 Excessive spam, scam bots, and unformatted text walls are strictly prohibited.
Article V: The Supreme Authority
Sec. 1 The Führer holds the supreme power; the other admins maintain order and discipline.
Sec. 2 All final decisions regarding group survival, structural changes, and pardons rest solely with the Führer.
Sec. 3 Admins serve at the pleasure of the Führer, while citizens fuel the chat's survival.
Article VI: Emergency Clause
Sec. 1 Only Admins may invoke a State of Emergency during group crises.
Sec. 2 All normal chat rules are suspended until the Führer or Admins lift the decree.
